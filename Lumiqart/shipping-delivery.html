Shipping & Delivery

Shipping information for the Lumiqart e-commerce project.

Processing

Orders are prepared after confirmation. Processing time can vary depending on product availability.

Delivery

Estimated delivery time is normally 3–7 business days after dispatch. Remote locations may take longer.

For this demo website, order tracking can be connected to a courier API in a future version.
